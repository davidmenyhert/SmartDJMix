SmartDJ Mix

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

SmartDJ Mix is a mobile DJ mixing application for playing and mixing music from supported files, with optional USB audio interfaces and Bluetooth audio output. This policy explains how information is handled when using the app.

Information stored on your device

SmartDJ Mix stores application settings and music-library information locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this can include playlists, selected file references, imported audio copies, mixer and audio-routing preferences, and other configuration values needed to restore your setup.

When you choose music from your phone, an attached USB storage device, or a supported document provider, the app accesses the files you select. Some selected music files may be copied into app-managed local storage so they remain available to the music library. SmartDJ Mix does not operate a server to receive your personal music collection or playlists.

Audio, files, and device permissions

Depending on the Android version and enabled features, SmartDJ Mix may request access to audio files or user-selected folders, or permission to use an audio input. Such access is used for the app's music, recording, or audio-diagnostic functions, where available.

Connected USB audio devices and available output routes may be identified to let you choose MASTER and MONITOR outputs. Bluetooth routing is handled through Android's audio system. This routing information is used on the device to configure audio playback.

Audio input, when used by a supported feature, is processed locally. Simply using the app does not send microphone or USB audio input to a SmartDJ Mix-operated server.

Recordings

If you choose to record a mix using the app's REC feature, the resulting audio file is saved to storage on your device. The recording is not automatically uploaded to the developer. You decide whether to keep, export, share, or delete it. Recordings saved to a shared Music folder may remain after the app is uninstalled, depending on Android's storage behavior.

Bluetooth, external audio interfaces, and DJ SILENT

SmartDJ Mix can route its audio to the phone's output, a compatible Bluetooth device, or supported USB audio-interface outputs. Some modes allow separate MASTER and headphone MONITOR routes when supported by your device and audio hardware.

The optional DJ SILENT controls may open or use Android's system settings for notification interruptions and touch sounds, with the permissions and user choices required by Android. SmartDJ Mix does not need to read the contents of your calls, messages, or notifications to offer these controls.

Hardware direct monitoring, when supported by a connected audio interface, is performed by the external hardware rather than by a SmartDJ Mix cloud service.

Advertising and third-party services

The version of SmartDJ Mix described by this policy does not include an in-app advertising or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not require a SmartDJ Mix account for local DJ mixing.

If you select a file from a third-party document or cloud-storage provider, that provider may handle authentication and file transfer under its own privacy policy. SmartDJ Mix does not independently receive your account password for such a provider.

If advertising, analytics, cloud accounts, or music-streaming integrations are introduced in a future release, this policy will be updated to explain any additional processing.

Internet access

Basic playback and mixing of music already available on your device do not require a SmartDJ Mix online account. Accessing files through a cloud document provider can require an internet connection and is governed by that provider's service. The app does not currently provide a built-in streaming-service login described by this policy.

Children's privacy

SmartDJ Mix is not specifically designed for children. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users' access to music, recording features, and connected services as appropriate.

Your choices and controls

  • Choose which audio files, folders, and external devices the app can access.
  • Change or revoke applicable permissions through Android settings.
  • Manage audio-routing and DJ SILENT preferences through the app and Android settings.
  • Delete imported copies, playlists, saved recordings, and other local data using the relevant app or device storage controls.

Data security and retention

App-managed settings, imported audio copies, and playlist information are kept on your device until removed through the app, your device's storage settings, or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and storage behavior. Files stored in shared or user-selected folders may need to be deleted separately.

Because SmartDJ Mix does not operate a cloud account service for these local features, it does not provide server-side retrieval or deletion of a personal music library.

Updates to this policy

This Privacy Policy may change if SmartDJ Mix gains new features, uses additional services, or changes how information is processed. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information provided on the SmartDJ Mix Google Play Store listing.
